## Copyright and knowledge boundary

- Manuals are human reference material for independently written principles and workflows, not source text for copying or prompt stuffing.
- Source rights are classified as `owned`, `licensed`, `public_domain`, `permissioned`, `unknown`, or `restricted`; unknown/restricted fail closed.
- Generated TypeScript is never injected into the kernel from a manual parser. Approved declarative Rule DSL, schema validation, deterministic tests, and human approval are required.
- Proprietary geometry is not indexed or transformed without explicit allowed-use rights. Retrieval returns generalized patterns and provenance references, not unauthorized copies.

## Multi-agent boundary

MADR is bounded by rounds, time, calls, cost, concurrency, retries, and cancellation. Agent consensus is concept evidence only and cannot issue exact/manufacturing verification. Missing consensus or evidence becomes `NEEDS_INPUT` or `NOT_RUN`.
